head is for upper info

body is for the main content

semantic tags describe meaning and purpose. 
header contains introductory content or nav links
nav contains navigation links to other pages/sections
main is main content of the pages section is a distinct section of content
article is independent content
aside is related but separate from main content
footer contains closing info like contact or copyright
h1,h2,h3,h4,h5,h6 are different levels of headings

non sematic do not provide information about meaning of their content. their job is styling and layout purpose.
div is for grouping a large section of a page to stylize
<div class="fish">
span stylize a section of content.
<p> yada <span class="egg"> dada </span> bada </p>

semantic tags are best in general case.

non semantic tags are for stylizing things.

h1-main title, use only one per page.
h2-major section headings that divide in to main parts
h3-even smaller parts of h2.
h4-minor headings for h3
h5-very specific headings
h6- smallest only used if you really need detailed organization.
Use proper heading levels so screen readers don't break
